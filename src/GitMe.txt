There is no requirement in any cloud to have every component in an availability set.
Core Skype components in the cloud solution are placed in availability sets for high availability.
Boxes not in availability sets are designed that way because:

They serve a single function.
They are not intended to be highly available.


Examples: Windows Admin Centre, PowerShell boxes – quick to rebuild, so HA is unnecessary.
Adding them to availability sets would increase cost without benefit.
The assumption that “all production services have availability sets” comes from an application-level perspective, not from auxiliary services.
Exceptions may exist, but 99.9% follow this design principle.
